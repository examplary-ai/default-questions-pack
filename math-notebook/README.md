The math notebook question type gives students a notebook-style interface where they work through a problem line by line, in real mathematical notation, with the final line as their answer. It's built for "show your work" questions: you see the whole derivation, not just the result.

## When to use it

- Multi-step algebra: solving equations, simplifying expressions, rewriting formulas.
- Calculus and physics derivations where the route matters as much as the destination.
- Any question where you want to award partial credit for correct intermediate steps.

For questions where only the final expression matters, the simpler formula question type may be a better fit.

## How it works for students

The student starts with a few empty lines (configurable) and adds steps as needed, each entered in a math input field that renders proper notation. The last line is treated as the final answer. Plan for roughly 6 minutes per question.

## Grading

Graded by AI: both the final answer and the intermediate steps are considered against your scoring criteria, with semantic comparison of expressions (`2/4` and `1/2` count as equivalent). This makes it easy to award partial credit for sound working with a slip at the end. You review and can adjust all suggested scores.

## Settings

| Setting       | Description                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial lines | How many empty lines the notebook starts with (default 4). |
