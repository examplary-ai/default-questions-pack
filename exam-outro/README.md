The outro block is the final page of a test. It isn't a question — it's a closing text shown after the last question, ideal for submission instructions and a friendly sign-off.

## When to use it

- Reminding students to review their answers before submitting.
- Explaining what happens next: when results are published, how feedback is shared.
- Thanking students for their effort and closing the test on a positive note.

## How it works for students

The outro is always shown at the end of the test (its position is enforced), with the title "Outro". Students read it and submit; there is nothing to answer.
