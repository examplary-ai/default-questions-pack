The voice recorder question type asks students to answer by speaking: they record their response directly in the browser. It brings oral skills into written tests — pronunciation, fluency, and the ability to explain something out loud.

## When to use it

- Language learning: pronunciation practice, reading aloud, spoken responses.
- Oral explanations: "Explain in your own words how photosynthesis works."
- Presentations and reflections where hearing the student matters.

## How it works for students

The student clicks record, speaks their answer, and can listen back before submitting. You set a maximum recording length appropriate to the task — 60–120 seconds suits short spoken answers, up to 300 seconds for longer oral explanations. Students need a device with a microphone. Plan for roughly 5 minutes per question.

## Grading

The recording is transcribed and graded by AI against your scoring criteria, with your review. You can also listen to the original recording while grading — useful when pronunciation or delivery is part of the assessment.

## Settings

| Setting                | Description                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Max duration (seconds) | The maximum recording length (default 300). |
